## Generation prompts

### 1. Emblem — transparent

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original production brand emblem for Royal Rose Studio by Gagan, an artistic makeup and bridal artistry business with a Velvet Rose visual identity. Design ONE exceptional, distinctive logo emblem, NO TEXT. The idea is a rose and makeup brush fused into ONE flowing drawn symbol, not separate clip-art objects placed together. A sophisticated open rose bloom built from 6–8 broad, sculptural calligraphic petals in deep burgundy, with generous transparent negative spaces and intentional natural asymmetry. The lower petal gracefully becomes a single tapered makeup-brush handle extending diagonally down-right; a small refined antique-gold ferrule connects the handle into the petal/bristle form. The brush gesture and the rose must feel inevitable and beautifully integrated. A subtly implied capital R can occur in the negative space, but never force letters or a complicated monogram. Confident illustrator-drawn silhouette, exquisite proportions, crisp vector-like edges, deliberate curves, elegant boutique identity designer finish. Flat spot-colour artwork: deep wine #6d233f with a restrained antique-gold #bfa184 accent, optionally one dusty-rose #c88d98 inner petal. It must be usable at 48px and printable in one ink. Centered compact emblem, nearly square composition, fill 80–86% of canvas, even clean margins. Genuinely transparent background, preserve alpha; no painted background. No mockup, no paper, no scene, no gradient, no 3D, no metallic rendering, no tiny hairlines, no realistic flower, no crowns, no eyelashes, no faces, no lips, no additional tools, no border, no circular badge, no sparkles, no watermark. Make the symbol artistically memorable and carefully edited rather than decorative or busy.

### 2. Wordmark — transparent

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final complete logo image for Royal Rose Studio by Gagan. Image 1 is the approved rose-and-makeup-brush emblem and is the edit target to preserve. Create ONE carefully typeset horizontal brand lockup on a genuinely transparent background. Preserve the exact emblem design, recognisable petal geometry, brush handle, proportions and burgundy/dusty-rose/antique-gold palette of Image 1; only scale and position it to the left of the lettering. To its right, add the exact words "Royal Rose" on one line in exquisite high-contrast fashion serif lettering, with bespoke-looking R terminals, beautifully balanced letter spacing and confident editorial proportions. Beneath, a refined small-caps "STUDIO" with wide tracking, a short antique-gold divider, and "by Gagan" in a graceful readable serif italic. Exact text only: "Royal Rose", "STUDIO", "by Gagan". No other text. Main lettering deep burgundy #6d233f, small typography also burgundy, tiny divider antique gold #bfa184. The mark and lettering must feel like one balanced professional bridal makeup identity with generous but controlled negative space. WIDE horizontal 3:1 composition, requested canvas 1536 x 512, artwork occupies approximately 88 percent of the width and 80 percent of the height, clean even outer padding, no giant empty margins. The emblem's bloom sits naturally level with the wordmark; no overlapping text. Crisp polished graphic artwork, print-clean, authentic specialist identity-design finish. Transparent alpha around all elements. No paper, mockup, shadows, scenery, material textures, extra icons, extra ornaments, no watermark, no checkerboard drawn in.
