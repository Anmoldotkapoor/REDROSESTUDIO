# Royal Rose Academy

Royal Rose Academy is a dedicated static page at `academy.html`, linked from the Studio desktop and mobile navigation, homepage invitation and footer. It offers four designed learning paths personally taught by Gagan: The Personal Beauty Edit, The Artistry Foundation, The Bridal Atelier and The Beauty Circle. Path formats distinguish one-to-one learning, group learning and paths that can be discussed in either format.

No prices, schedules, hours, fixed venues, class-size limits, qualifications or certificates are invented. Session dates, venue, availability and pricing are confirmed directly with Gagan. Enquiries cover Yuba City, Lincoln, Sacramento, Elk Grove and Surrey, BC, Canada.

## Interaction and enquiry behaviour

- Filters expose personal, artistry and group paths; each pack has curriculum details and a distinct editorial photograph, also used in its detail dialog.
- Choosing a path or city carries those choices to the enquiry planner. Unsupported format combinations are corrected to a format that path offers.
- WhatsApp and email links use the supplied business contacts and carry the selected path, format, city, experience and optional learning goals. They request availability, course arrangements and pricing details.
- The visitor reviews and sends the message. The site does not send messages, take payments, reserve places or store enquiry text. Only the existing theme and motion preferences use local storage.
- The animated brush study has play/pause and three manual movements. Motion-off and reduced-motion preferences disable automatic playback, while manual exploration remains available. Playback pauses off-screen, on modal use and when the page is hidden.

## Verification

- Seven Academy tests cover path/format compatibility, preference carry-through, all course/city contact combinations, encoded free text, unknown values and input limits, distinct image paths, static course content and absence of price amounts.
- Browser checks at 320, 360, 390, 430, 768, 900, 1024 and 1440px in both themes: no document overflow, header overflow or clipping in the title, course content, planner or brush study. The actual viewport width was verified for each check.
- Filters return 1 personal, 2 artistry, 3 group and 4 total paths. All four curriculum dialogs show the matching image and four learning topics; the detail dialog fits at 320px.
- A group Bridal Atelier enquiry for Surrey correctly carries experience and free text to both WhatsApp and email. Switching to the Personal Beauty Edit resets the unsupported group format to one-to-one. Choosing Foundations from the group filter preserves group learning and the selected Sacramento location.
- The animation advances to Blend, manual Refine changes the study, motion-off disables playback, and manual Place remains usable. The phone menu opens and closes with Escape.
- Phone navigation from Academy to Studio and back succeeds, preserving the theme. All four offer images load. HTML structure, ARIA targets, cross-page anchors, modules, styles and image references are checked for both pages. No external messages were sent.
- Checks use the available browser at phone/tablet/desktop viewport sizes and automated tests; physical iOS/Android hardware was not available.
