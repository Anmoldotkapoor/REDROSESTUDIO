THE LITTLE DETAILS

Before we begin.

Which occasions can I enquire about?+

Bridal makeup, Jago, Mehndi and pre-wedding photoshoot makeup. If your celebrations span several events, include them in one enquiry so Gagan can discuss your plans with you.

Where does Royal Rose Studio serve?+

Yuba City, Lincoln, Sacramento, Elk Grove and Surrey, BC, Canada. Include your venue or getting-ready location in your enquiry to discuss location arrangements.

How do I check my date and get a quote?+

Use the enquiry builder below, call 916-217-9247, or email bookings.royalrosebygagan@outlook.com. Your enquiry starts a conversation; availability, pricing and booking details are confirmed directly with Gagan.

What should I include in my enquiry?+

Your date, occasion, city and preferred ready-by time are a lovely starting point. Share the colors of your outfit, inspiration images, and any skin sensitivities or product preferences you would like to discuss.

Can I see real client work?+

Yes — explore @royalrosestudiobygagan on Instagram ↗. The Instagram gallery shows previews from our own Instagram posts. Elsewhere, AI-created editorial inspiration is labelled accordingly.
