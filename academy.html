THE LITTLE DETAILS

Before your
first brushstroke.

Do I need makeup experience?+

There are starting points for learning your own makeup, exploring foundations and developing your artistry. Share your experience and goals with Gagan so she can discuss a suitable path with you.

Can I learn one-to-one or in a group?+

Yes. The Personal Beauty Edit is a one-to-one path; The Beauty Circle is for group learning. The Artistry Foundation and The Bridal Atelier can be discussed as one-to-one or group training.

Where can I enquire about training?+

Yuba City, Lincoln, Sacramento, Elk Grove and Surrey, BC, Canada. Gagan will confirm available dates, venue and arrangements directly with you.

How do I find out the course price?+

Choose a path and use WhatsApp or email to request the complete course and pricing details from Gagan. Prices are shared personally; no booking or payment is taken on this website.

What should I bring?+

Tell Gagan about the tools and products you already use and the skills you want to learn. She’ll confirm what to bring and the preparation for your chosen training.

How do I arrange my session?+

Send your learning enquiry to Gagan. Together, discuss the path, learning format, city, dates and pricing before making any arrangements. An enquiry does not reserve or confirm a place.
